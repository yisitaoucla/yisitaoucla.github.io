About

I build the systems that help video start reliably and play smoothly at scale.

At Instagram, I led the migration of the iOS video loading, prefetching, and caching stack. The migration reduced disk writes and made the architecture reusable across teams. I also defined session-level playback metrics and worked with Android engineers to adopt them as shared performance goals.

My work connects mobile architecture, networking, and product delivery, from video performance in Reels, Feed, and Stories to Picture-in-Picture and offline viewing.
